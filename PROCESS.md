process
I used Chatgpt as a writing and discussion partner throughout the brainstorm， drafting and development of this essay.

I first described my own background. Based on this information and the assignment requirements, the AI helped me develop an initial structure around the question of why designers should learn programming when AI can increasingly generate code.

Then I kept the idea of treating programming as a creative material rather than only a technical skill from the AI's suggestions.

This was useful because it gave me a clearer way to connect my Industrial Design background with my current interest in game design. I am already used to thinking about materials through making and experimentation, but I had previously thought about code mainly as something used to implement a finished design. The idea helped me change my mind and make my direction clear.

And I rejected some of the AI's earlier, more general points about why designers should learn programming. For example, it initially suggested ideas such as programming helping designers communicate better with developers.

I did not think these points were specific enough to my own experience. They could apply to almost any design student and did not really answer why I should learn programming.

Instead, I replaced these points with my own experience using Unreal Engine 5. This felt more specific to my situation, and it led to the final argument that programming knowledge gives me the ability to understand and experiment with the systems I design, even when AI is doing some of the implementation.
